## What we are actually offering

Sense6 is a free, privacy-first collection of **recreational browser perception
experiments**, not calibrated medical, occupational or population-ranking tests.
The most distinctive educational resources are:

- [Classroom reaction-time investigation](../../public/education/browser-reaction-time/index.html)
  — 25 minutes, printable fictional observations, median versus mean,
  secondary-level science, computing and media literacy. It neither asks for
  student identities nor uploads their results.
- [Browser Timing Explorer](https://sense6.net/en/browser-timing-explorer/)
  — a 20-minute model of two **hypothetical** setups, with separately displayed
  refresh-interval arithmetic; it never measures or calibrates a device.
- [Reaction-time research explanation](https://sense6.net/en/reaction-time-guide/)
  — answers a specific search question and explains uncertainty with references.

Do not advertise a diagnosis, evidence of a faster person, a calibrated
benchmark, human validation, or clinical suitability.

## Publication and reuse gate

The live lesson explicitly permits **printing and sharing the unaltered
worksheet with students for classroom learning**, retaining the creator,
context and safety notes. The printed worksheet includes those notices.
This is distinct from the repository's MIT **software** license.

The website has **not** granted a blanket Creative Commons license for the
lesson or permission to sell, adapt or republish it. Do not select a Creative
Commons license in external forms or assert one exists without the copyright
holder's separate decision. Before uploading or submitting a resource, verify
the published rights statement against the destination's actual policy.

A broader CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC 4.0 release might improve reuse, but it is an
explicit maintainer rights decision, not an SEO setting. Check the ownership of
all included text, figures and fonts before granting it.

## Measurement method

Use the already-active first-party Bronto Pulse resources. Do not add tags,
cookies, fingerprints, student response uploads or a second analytics service.

1. Confirm the exact deployed SHA, the live self-canonical response and a
   valid sitemap entry. IndexNow HTTP 200/202 means URL notification received,
   **not** crawl, index, ranking or an AI citation.
2. Google Search Console: inspect exact URLs for the classroom lesson,
   Timing Explorer, English reaction guide, German tetrachromacy guide and
   a localized experiment. Record indexed, excluded or unknown status and
   the actual crawl date. Compare *complete* equal-length windows; separate
   branded and nonbranded intents. Page/query samples are provider-limited.
3. Bing Webmaster Tools: inspect the sitemap and the AI Performance report.
   Record cited-page URLs and grouped grounding-query themes, if available.
   Citations are not visits or a ranking score.
4. Bronto Pulse: read Cloudflare page-level views/referrers and GSC page-level
   impressions/clicks for each new resource. Do not join visitors or interpret
   a pageview as an individual person. Annotate release outages separately.
5. First-time visits: ask 5–8 consenting volunteers to find the lesson,
   printable worksheet, model controls and real experiment. Record only
   non-identifying friction and whether limits are understood, using the
   existing [field kit](first-visitor-field-kit.md).
6. Human language review: begin with German tetrachromacy queries showing
   actual GSC exposure; then review English source clarity and additional
   locales as data warrants. Automated locale checks are not native review.

### Decision rules

- Search exposure but no clicks: inspect the query, the exact page result,
  intent mismatch and title snippet before rewriting copy.
- Indexed and clicked but few observed page views: account for collection
  differences and privacy exclusions; never infer a broken conversion funnel
  from aggregate providers.
- Referral interest without comprehension: simplify early choices and safe
  explanations, backed by consented human observations.
- Zero verified external citations: publish reusable original educational
  work and seek authentic education listings, not invented schema, bulk AI
  pages, paid backlinks or automated AI citation checks.
